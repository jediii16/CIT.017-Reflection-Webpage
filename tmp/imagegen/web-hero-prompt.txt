Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: responsive website landing-page comic artwork
Primary request: Create an entirely original superhero-comic illustration that captures the broad energy of a dynamic web-swinging city comic without depicting or copying Spider-Man or any existing copyrighted character.
Scene/backdrop: A fictional modern city viewed from a dramatic low angle, with tall angular buildings, open pale-blue sky, and several sweeping strands of bright white synthetic webbing crossing the composition.
Subject: One original masked urban hero called the Thread Runner, swinging rapidly through the city. Athletic acrobatic pose with strong foreshortening, located mainly in the right third of the landscape frame so the center and left remain usable behind an interactive globe. The original suit uses crimson shoulder armor, deep navy torso panels, black utility seams, small gold angular eye lenses, and a short split scarf trailing behind. No chest emblem. The mask has a solid crimson crown and navy lower face, with no web pattern on the costume. The web line comes from a compact wrist tool rather than a biological power.
Style/medium: Hand-inked American superhero comic illustration, bold varied black outlines, crisp cel coloring, dramatic perspective, vintage offset-print halftone shading, subtle imperfect ink registration, lightly weathered paper grain, energetic motion lines.
Composition/framing: 3:2 landscape. Hero on the right, swinging diagonally upward. Large open negative space through the center for a monochrome globe. Web arcs and skyline create a frame around that central space without crossing the hero's face.
Lighting/mood: Bright daytime, adventurous, kinetic, optimistic, high contrast.
Color palette: Crimson red, deep navy blue, black ink, pale cyan sky, white web strands, small warm-yellow accents.
Constraints: No text anywhere. No logo. No watermark. No trademarked symbols. No recognizable real-world buildings. Keep the central area relatively low-detail and readable beneath a UI overlay.
Avoid: Spider-Man, Peter Parker, Miles Morales, Marvel branding, spider emblems, large white almond-shaped eyes, red-and-blue web-patterned bodysuits, exact costume panels from any existing superhero, copied pose or framing from the supplied reference, robotic spider enemies, copyrighted characters, comic-cover title typography.
